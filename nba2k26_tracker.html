⭐ 2-WAY PLAYER RATING SYSTEM
Balanced 50/50 split between offense and defense. Each component is weighted to reward different aspects of play, with benchmarks calibrated to rec league performance.
OFFENSIVE WEIGHT: 50 points total
Scoring 40% • Playmaking 20% • Efficiency 20% • Shooting 10% • Ball Security 10%
DEFENSIVE WEIGHT: 50 points total
Prevention 50% • Steals 20% • Blocks 20% • Rebounding 10%
🏀 REC LEAGUE CALIBRATED: Stat benchmarks adjusted to realistic rec center performance levels
RATING TIERS
90-100: Elite
80-89: All-Star
70-79: Starter
60-69: Role Player
Below 60: Developing
📌 HOW IT WORKS
Weights (max points per category) determine how much each stat category matters. These are fixed.
Benchmarks (stats needed for max) determine what performance is required. These are calibrated to rec league averages.

Example: Scoring is weighted at 20 points max (40% of offense). The benchmark is 25 PPG to earn all 20 points. At 18 PPG, you'd earn 14.4 points (18÷25×20).
OFFENSIVE RATING (0-50)
Weights: 40% scoring, 20% playmaking, 20% efficiency, 10% shooting, 10% ball security
Scoring: Weight: 20 pts (40%)
Formula: PPG ÷ 22 × 20
Benchmark: 22 PPG earns full 20 points
Playmaking: Weight: 10 pts (20%)
Formula: APG ÷ 6 × 10
Benchmark: 6 APG earns full 10 points
FG% Efficiency: Weight: 10 pts (20%)
Formula: FG% ÷ 100 × 10
Benchmark: 100% FG earns full 10 points
3P% Shooting: Weight: 5 pts (10%)
Formula: 3P% ÷ 100 × 5
Benchmark: 100% 3P earns full 5 points
Ball Security: Weight: 5 pts (10%)
Formula: AST/TO ÷ 4.5 × 5
Benchmark: 4.5 ratio earns full 5 points
DEFENSIVE RATING (0-50)
Weights: 50% prevention, 20% steals, 20% blocks, 10% rebounding
Points Prevention: Weight: 25 pts (50%)
Formula: (30 - PAPG) ÷ 15 × 25
Benchmark: 15 PAPG = 25 pts (max), 30+ PAPG = 0 pts
Steals: Weight: 10 pts (20%)
Formula: SPG ÷ 2 × 10
Benchmark: 2 SPG earns full 10 points
Blocks: Weight: 10 pts (20%)
Formula: BPG ÷ 1.8 × 10
Benchmark: 1.8 BPG earns full 10 points
Rebounding: Weight: 5 pts (10%)
Formula: RPG ÷ 10 × 5
Benchmark: 10 RPG earns full 5 points

💥 IMPACT SCORE
Per-game performance metric - Measures total contribution in a single game using weighted stats. Unlike the 2-Way Rating (which evaluates overall player quality), Impact Score directly rewards/penalizes each action.
FORMULA
PTS×1.0 + REB×1.2 + AST×1.5 + STL×2 + BLK×2
+ FG%×0.5 + 3P%×0.3
- TO×2 - PA×1.0
POSITIVE CONTRIBUTIONS
Points (×1.0): Baseline scoring value
Rebounds (×1.2): Possession value
Assists (×1.5): Creates opportunities
Steals (×2.0): Transition opportunities
Blocks (×2.0): Rare, game-changing
FG% (×0.5): Efficiency bonus
3P% (×0.3): Shooting bonus
PENALTIES
Turnovers (×2.0): Possession loss
Points Allowed (×1.0): Symmetric with scoring
💡 EXAMPLE GAME
Player Performance: 20 PTS, 8 REB, 6 AST, 2 STL, 1 BLK, 2 TO, 18 PA, 55% FG (7/12), 40% 3P (2/5)
Calculation:
+20 (pts) +9.6 (reb) +9 (ast) +4 (stl) +2 (blk) +27.5 (FG%) +12 (3P%)
-4 (TO) -18 (PA)
Impact Score: +62.1

vs 2-Way Rating: Impact Score measures game-by-game performance. High-usage players who turn it over but stuff the stat sheet will have high Impact Scores. The 2-Way Rating evaluates overall player quality using AST/TO ratio instead of direct TO penalties. PA penalty is 1.0 (symmetric with scoring) - allowing 20 points = giving up 20 points of value.
